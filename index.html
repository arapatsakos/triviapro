Trivia Pro

A trivia game for iPhone: quick rounds, a map of game modes, and Party games with friends.

Support

Questions, a wrong answer you spotted, or a problem? Email arapatsakos@gmail.com. For a question that looks wrong, include the question text so we can check it.

Purchases

  • Coins and XP packs are one-time purchases used inside the game.
  • Trivia Pass is a monthly or yearly subscription with a free trial for new subscribers. It renews automatically until you cancel in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the renewal date.
  • New phone? Open Shop and tap Restore purchases.

Party games

  • Same room: one phone taps Host a game, the others join. Keep Wi-Fi or Bluetooth on and allow Local Network access when asked.
  • Online: tap Play online and sign in to Game Center to invite friends or get matched.

Privacy

Trivia Pro collects no personal data. Read the Privacy Policy.
