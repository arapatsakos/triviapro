Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Trivia Pro does not collect, store or share any personal data. We have no servers and no accounts.

What the app stores

Your progress, coins, XP, skills, badges and settings are saved only on your device.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple handles your nickname, leaderboard scores, achievements and online matches under Apple's own privacy policy. We never receive your Apple Account details.

Party games

In a same-room Party game, phones connect directly to each other over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth (encrypted). Only the name you type, your chosen color, the questions and the answers are exchanged, and only during the game.

Purchases

Coins, XP packs, the Starter Pack and the Trivia Pass are handled by Apple's App Store. Apple processes any payment; we never see your payment details.

No tracking

No analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
